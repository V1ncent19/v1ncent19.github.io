hi, there.

I am a Ph.D. student in Statistics at the Department of Statistics and Data Science at Northwestern University. Prior to this, I received my B.S. degree in Mathematics and Physics from the Department of Physics at Tsinghua University in 2023, accompanied by a minor B.S. in Statistics.

Both disciplines of theoretical Physics and high-dimensional Statistics captivate me. Here, I record working notes, mathematical derivations, and open-source course digests such as the Statistics summary notes and the THU Stat Wiki repository.

Beyond mathematical rigor, this archive mirrors an intellectual cabinet of curiosities—spanning philology, culinary craft (l'art culinaire), and travel stamps. Please inspect the index below or browse topics directly.

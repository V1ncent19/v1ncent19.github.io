Atelier du vitrail brings my travel stories into a floating stained-glass garden. Each window takes its image from a journey. Explore the garden, choose a window for a closer look, then click it again to open the story.

Daylight and moonlight change with the location selected on the world map. The glass and its coloured projections shift together, while the central tree sways and petals drift across the stone.

<!-- zh -->

Atelier du vitrail 是一座用彩窗陈列游记的浮空花园。每扇窗的图案都来自旅途中的照片。可以自由调整视角探索，点选花窗进入近景，再次点击便能阅读对应的游记。

在世界地图上选取位置，花园中的日光与月光也会随之改变。窗面的明暗与落在石盘上的彩光同步变化，中央的树轻轻摇曳，花瓣缓缓飘落。

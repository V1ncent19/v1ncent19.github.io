[feiyue.online](https://feiyue.online) 是清华大学飞跃手册的项目位置——一份由清华学生维护的长期社区项目，汇集学长学姐的申请经验与建议。我参与了这个项目网站的建设与维护。

The [Feiyue Handbook](https://feiyue.online) is a long-running community project run by Tsinghua students, collecting application experiences and advice from previous generations. I participate in building and maintaining the website.
